Simply open [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/goniy/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/sanju-portfolio/index.html) in your browser:

## 🔤 10 Modern Tech Fonts (Ubuntu Default)

The portfolio defaults to the **Ubuntu** humanist tech typeface. To switch to any of the 10 fonts:
1. Type **`admin`** on your keyboard to open Owner Mode.
2. Click **`⚙️ Admin Hub`** and go to the **`🔤 Fonts (10)`** tab.
3. Choose any font (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono, Space Grotesk, Sora, Fira Sans, etc.) to preview and save live.
